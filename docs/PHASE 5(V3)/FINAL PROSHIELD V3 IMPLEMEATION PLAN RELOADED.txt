One very important correction to the "game changer" idea

I agree that this could become a genuinely differentiated architecture, but I would not yet call it a market game changer as a factual claim.

The technically defensible claim today is:

ProShield is deliberately building a different security workflow: a beginner-accessible Blue Team/SOC platform where human investigation decisions become structured, provenance-rich security knowledge that can later be converted into versioned AI datasets.

That's a strong architecture.

Whether it is actually novel compared with existing products/research needs a separate market and literature comparison.
